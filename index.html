Silicon Valley culture is toxic.

Critically ask who benefits from discussions around endless growth and big datasets — and what you are being asked to surrender to achieve it.

Creative ML has
different goals.

Creators know deep engagement with materials and tools matter.

With creative AI, the materials are the dataset and the machine learning system.

As artists we need to work with machine learning systems in a way that does not mean surrendering our data or creative control.

Smaller data = less computationally intensive and more sustainable models.

But what about bias?

In one sense, the bias inherent in a small, manually-curated, well-understood dataset may be desirable.

When a creator employs a dataset as a medium for communicating an idea to the machine, they can better control the outputs they generate.

InteractML — visual scripting ML for Unreal.

Embodied ML: classification driving real-time lighting.

Artefacts and technologies embody cultural knowledge and mediate social interactions — enabling the accumulation and transmission of cultural information across generations.

In this way, datasets and the equitable sharing of them need to be a priority for research.

Project

Federated Data Commons
for Creative Communities

Mozilla Data Collective — Create. Curate. Control.

We aim to develop community-governed small-scale datasets for local training on low-resource models — empowering creators with greater control while reducing environmental impact.

Three key areas

  • Access
  • Sustainability
  • Regulation

Federation is a radical turn online. It divests ownership from centralised platforms to communities.

We will support small-scale, curator-made datasets for low-resource models — to address concerns about large AI models trained on poorly curated datasets without creator permission.

Output

Federated Data Commons
Governance Framework

Website prototype that establishes community-managed protocols and resources.

Public governance models will be born from testing with archives and communities.

  • Downloadable governance templates, checklists and documentation
  • Decision-support flow charts for selecting governance options
  • Customisable permission statements and dataset documentation templates
  • Implementation guides for establishing a federated data commons

Documentation and case studies based on our research will inform publications, a symposium, and a policy report.

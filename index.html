With creative AI, the materials are the dataset and the machine learning system.

REFUSAL

SUSTAINABILITY

Smaller data = less computationally intensive and more sustainable models.

THE OBJECTION

REFRAMING

AIM

Community-governed small-scale datasets for local training on low-resource models.

Empowering creators with greater control while reducing environmental impact.

SCOPE

Three key areas

Access
Who can build, train, and own creative AI.
Sustainability
Small data, low-resource models, degrowth-compatible compute.
Regulation
Governance that gives consent and refusal back to creators.
RADICAL TURN

Federation divests ownership from centralised platforms to communities.

APPROACH

Small-scale, curator-made datasets for low-resource models.

Addressing concerns about large AI models trained on poorly curated datasets without creator permission.

PROMPT 2 · DELIVERABLE:

The Antigravity Machine

This directly addresses the dominant attractor problem in generative AI.

Non-Linear Forking
Branch explorations at any point. Maintain multiple trajectories simultaneously rather than being funnelled toward a single resolved output.
Persistent Journey Mapping
Record and visualise the full exploration path. The creative process becomes visible, reviewable, resumable across sessions.
Output Indeterminacy Controls
Adjustable parameters that modulate the degree of resolution. Hold outputs in productive ambiguity rather than forcing premature convergence.
Dominant Attractor Diagnostics
Visualisation layer making convergence pressures legible. Showing practitioners where the system is pulling them and why.
